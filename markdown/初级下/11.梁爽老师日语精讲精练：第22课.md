1. V る/V た時（に）

Both the "verb ta-form" and the "verb dictionary form" can be followed by 時（に） to mean "at the time of
...". But note that the time points expressed by the "verb dictionary form" and the "verb ta-form" differ.
とき とき
"V る時（に）" refers to something that happens while the preceding action is not yet complete (example ①), while "V た時
（に）" refers to something that happens at the point after the preceding action is complete (examples ②③).

① 友達の家に遊びに行く時に、お土産を買いました。／When (I) was about to go to a friend's house

2. V たことがある <experience>
"Verb ta-form" followed by ことがある expresses having had a certain experience, translatable as "have (once)
...". The negative form of this pattern is "V たことがない" (example ②). Also, when answering "〜
たことがありますか", you can use ある or ない.

① あの人は、確か前に会ったことがあります。／That person — I think I've met
(them) before.

② 日本で歌舞伎を見たことがありません。／I have never seen kabuki in Japan.
たいいく じゅぎょう け が
③ A：体育の授業で怪我をしたことがありますか。／Have you ever been injured in PE class?

B：はい、あります。／Yes, I have.

④ A：着物を着たことがありますか。／Have you ever worn a kimono?

The "V たことがある" pattern is mostly used for experiences long past or once had; it is not used to nar-
rate recent experiences (example ⑤).

fore, the verb used for entering a room is 上がる. Besides 上がってください, an even more polite way to say "please come in"
あ
