2. The polite form of verbs and the masu-stem <V ます形>
When Japanese verbs are used, the polite suffix “ます” is added after the dictionary form.
When the verb takes the suffix “ます”, the verb's form changes according to its conjugation class;
this change is called “verb conjugation”.
The specific rules for the change before “ます” are as follows:
Group I (godan) verbs:
Change the final u-row kana of the verb to the corresponding i-row kana, then attach “ます”. For example:

ねが ねが

ある：ある→り＋ます＝あります
