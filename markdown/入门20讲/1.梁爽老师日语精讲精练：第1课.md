Common greetings (1)
1. こんにちは。⓪ 你好。
This is a very common Japanese greeting, used to greet people you don't know well during the daytime. Reply
with “こんにちは” as well.
やす
2. お休みなさい。⑥ 晚安。
This is also one of the most common Japanese greetings, used before going to bed at night, or when parting at an evening gathering.
やす
“お休みなさい” is fairly polite and formal; with family and friends you can just say “お
やす
休み”.
3. すみません。④ I'm sorry. / Excuse me.
This greeting is used in many situations: to apologize, to thank, or when you need someone's help,
you can also say “すみません” to get their attention.
4. わかりました。④ Understood.; I see.
Usually, when someone gives you instructions or provides useful information, you can respond with “わか
りました”.
