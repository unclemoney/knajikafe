Common greetings (2)
はじ
1. 初めまして。④ Nice to meet you. (first meeting)
Japanese people mostly use this greeting when meeting for the first time.
ねが
2. よろしく お願いします。⓪＋⑥ Please treat me well. / I look forward to working with you.
This phrase can be used on its own, or after “初めまして。” when meeting for the first time.
ひさ
3. お久しぶりです。⑦ Long time no see.
Use this when meeting a friend again after a long time.
あした
4. また明日。⓪ See you tomorrow.
The adverb “また” means “again”. When friends part, they often use “また” followed by
the time of the next meeting, or simply “またね。”, equivalent to “see you”.
つか さま
5. お疲れ様でした。⑦ Thank you for your hard work.
This is one of the more commonly used Japanese greetings. It is mostly used between equals, or from a superior to a subordinate; it is
generally not used toward superiors.
