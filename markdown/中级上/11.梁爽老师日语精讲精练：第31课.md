Grammar Notes
1. V るべきだ <should; ought to>
The dictionary form of a verb followed by "べきだ" expresses that one must or should do something; translate as "should/must ...".
Its negative form is "べきではない", meaning it is better not to do so; "should not ...".
When "する" takes "べきだ", it can be either "するべきだ" or "すべきだ".
としよ たい ことばづか はな
① お年寄りに対して、あんな言葉遣いで話すべきではない。／You shouldn't speak to elderly people
with language like that.
としよ たい い かた
② お年寄りに対して、あんな言い方をするべきではない。／You shouldn't talk to elderly people
in that manner.
だいがくじだい たいけん おも
③ 大学時代はいろいろな体験をすべきだと思う。／I think you should experience many things
during your university years.
がくせい がっこう まも
④ 学生は学校のルールを守るべきです。／Students should follow the school rules.
"V るべきだ" can also modify a following noun; when it does, use the form "V るべきN", for example:

なお
⑤ どこか直すべきところはありますか。／Is there anywhere that should be fixed?
かのじょ い ぜんぶ い
⑥ 彼女に言うべきことを全部言った。／I said everything I ought to say to her.
2. 〜わりに（は）
Pattern: V attributive form ＋わりに（は）
A attributive form ＋わりに（は）
Ⅰ
A attributive form ＋わりに（は）
Ⅱ
N＋の＋わりに（は）
The attributive form of a verb or adjective, or "N＋の", followed by "わりに（は）" expresses that the result
in the latter clause does not match what would naturally be expected from the former. Translate as "...yet...", "for..."

4. V ないで
The nai-form of a verb followed by "で" is the negative continuative form: doing the latter action
without doing the former, or the latter happening instead. Translate as "without ...-ing".
ごだんかいひょうか つか こた しつもん
① 5段階評価を使わないで、もっと答えやすい質問にする。／Don't use the 5-point rating scale; replace it with questions that are easier to answer.

③ 子供が来ないで、親が来た。／The child didn't come; the parents came instead.

Pattern: A / A / V plain form ＋なら

Ⅱ
We learned earlier that a noun followed by "なら" highlights a topic (Lesson 15). Besides nouns,

8. 〜おかげだ。<cause of a positive result>・〜せいだ。<cause of a negative result>
Pattern: V attributive form ＋おかげだ（・せいだ）.
A / A attributive form ＋おかげだ（・せいだ）.
Ⅰ Ⅱ
N＋の＋おかげだ（・せいだ）。
The attributive form of a verb/adjective, or "noun+の", followed by "おかげだ" expresses the cause of
a positive result. It often appears as "〜おかげで、〜", with the latter clause describing how the speaker
concretely benefited. Translate as "thanks to...".
ぜんぶ さとう おし
① 全部佐藤さんが教えてくれたおかげです。／It's all thanks to you, Sato, for teaching me.
きょうりょく はっぴょう
② みんなが 協 力 してくれたおかげで、発表 がうまくいった。／thanks to; owing to
Thanks to everyone's help, the presentation went smoothly.
In contrast to "〜おかげだ", the attributive form or "noun+の" followed by "せいだ" expresses the cause of
a negative result. It can also appear as "〜せいで、〜" or "〜せいじゃない", meaning "it's all ...'s fault",
"just because of...", or "it's not ...'s fault".
こんかい しっぱい
③ 今回の失敗は、すべてわたしのせいです。／This failure is entirely my fault.
きみ おく さんがつ かんせい
④ 君が遅れたせいで、プロジェクトは3 月までに完成できなかった。／
Because of your delays, the project wasn't finished before March.
