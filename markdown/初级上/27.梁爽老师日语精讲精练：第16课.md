Grammar Notes
い
1. V masu-stem + に + 行く/来る/帰る <purposeful movement>
The particle "に" follows the verb masu-stem or an action noun (for suru-verbs, just the stem)
and is followed by a movement verb to express the purpose of the movement. Can be translated as "go (to a place) to ..."
do ... (an action)".
きょうしつ わす もの と い
① 教室に忘れ物を取りに行きます。／Go to the classroom to pick up something left behind.
なかま はなみ い
② サークルの仲間たちとお花見に行きます。／Go cherry-blossom viewing with my club friends.
きのうなかむら うち あそ い
③ 昨日中村さんのお家に遊びに行きました。／Yesterday I went to Nakamura's place to hang out.
さんぽ で
④ 散歩に出かけましょうか。／Shall we go out for a walk?
しりょう とど き
⑤ 資料を届けに来ました。／(I) came to deliver the materials.
2. V ます形＋ながら、〜
"ながら" follows the verb masu-stem and indicates that two actions happen simultaneously or keep alternating,
equivalent to "... while ..." in English.

3. Quantifier + も <subjectively many>
A quantifier followed by the particle "も" shows the speaker subjectively considers the amount large. In example ①, un-
der normal circumstances three students is not many, but when the speaker feels three students is a lot,

"も" can be added after the quantifier "3人" to convey the speaker's subjective sense that the number
is large.

① 教室に学生が3人もいます。／There are (as many as) three students in the classroom.

③ 日系企業を５社も訪問しました。／(We) visited (as many as) five Japanese companies.

6. 〜の（／ん）です。 <explanation II (verbs)>
Pattern: verb plain form + の（／ん）です。
In Lesson 12 we learned the form of "〜の（／ん）です。" following nouns and adjectives,
used to explain a situation. This lesson covers the form with the verb plain form followed by "〜の（／ん）で
す。"
Besides the three specific usage scenarios and functions learned in Lesson 12, "〜の（／ん）です" can also
be used in questions to ask the listener to explain the question raised. In this case, the preceding context usually
