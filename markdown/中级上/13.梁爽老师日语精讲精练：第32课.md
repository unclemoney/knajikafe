Grammar Notes
1. V（よ）う <volitional form of verbs>
The volitional form of a verb expresses the speaker's will, a suggestion, etc. Its conjugation rules are as follows:
Group I (godan) verbs:
Change the final u-dan kana of the verb to the corresponding o-dan kana and add "う". For example:
かえ かえ
返す：かえす→そ＋う＝返そう
か か
書く：かく→こ＋う＝書こう
いそ いそ
急ぐ：いそぐ→ご＋う＝急ごう
ま ま
待つ：まつ→と＋う＝待とう
よ よ
読む：よむ→も＋う＝読もう
あそ あそ
遊ぶ：あそぶ→ぼ＋う＝遊ぼう
おく おく
送る：おくる→ろ＋う＝送ろう
Group II (ichidan) verbs:
Drop the final kana "る" of the verb and add "よう". For example:
や や
辞める：やめる＋よう＝辞めよう
ね ね
寝る：ねる＋よう＝寝よう
Group III (irregular) verbs:
(1) Suru-verbs: change "する" to "し" and add "よう". That is:

3. 〜たら、〜た。 <discovery>
Pattern: V た + ら
Aい→かった+ら

We learned earlier that verbs, nouns, and adjectives followed by "たら" form a conditional. When the main
clause ends in the past tense — the "〜たら、〜た" pattern — it expresses that when the event before "たら"
occurred, the result in the latter clause turned out differently from what was expected. The latter clause is often
something beyond the speaker's control, a new discovery, or a surprise.

Pattern: N＋の＋ため（に）、〜

8. V ずに
Pattern: V ない＋ずに
To attach "ずに" to a verb, use the continuative of the negative form: drop the "ない" of the nai-form;
for the Group III verb "する", it becomes "せず" before "ず".
"V ずに" is the written form of "Vないで" and modifies the following clause adverbially:
doing the latter action without (doing) the former.
いちにち やす いっしょうけんめいはたら
① 一日も休まずに、一生懸命働いている。／(He) works incredibly hard without taking a single day off.
しごと だれ て つだ じぶん かんせい
② この仕事は誰にも手伝ってもらわずに、自分で完成させた。／I completed this work
on my own, without help from anyone.
なつやす い いえ しごと おも
③ この夏休みはどこにも行かずに、家で仕事しようと思う。／This summer vacation
