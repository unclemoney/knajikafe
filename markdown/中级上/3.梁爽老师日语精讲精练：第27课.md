ぶんぽうかいせつ
Grammar Notes
1. 〜といっても、〜
Pattern: plain sentence / noun だ ＋といっても、〜
A noun or the plain form of a verb/adjective followed by "といっても" forms a concessive clause. The main
clause qualifies or corrects the content of the subordinate clause, expressing that in reality the thing does not
reach the degree the clause describes — "although...; even though...; ...but really...".
ちか こうえん こうえん ちい にわ
① ホテルの近くに公園があります。公園といっても、小さなお庭です。
／There's a park near the hotel. Though calling it a park, it's really just a small courtyard.
がくひ たか はら たか
② 学費は高いといっても、払えないほど高くありません。／Although tuition is
expensive, it's not so expensive that you can't pay it.
く じつ さんかげつ
③ アフリカで暮らしたことがあるといっても、実は３ヶ月だけなんです。
／Although I lived in Africa, it was actually only three months.
2. 〜か〜
In Japanese, a plain-form question ending with the particle "か" can be embedded in another sentence,
becoming part of it. Note that a noun or na-adjective stem can directly take the question particle "か".
しょうがくきん おぼ
① いつ奨学金をもらったか覚えていますか。／Do you remember when you
received the scholarship?
じしん とき ひなん おし
② 地震の時、どこへ避難するか教えてください。／Please tell me where to evacuate
in the event of an earthquake.
とくい か
③ どんなスポーツが得意か書いてください。／Please write down which sports
you are good at.

3. V るようになる <change>
The dictionary form of a verb followed by "ようになる" expresses change: gaining an ability (from being unable
to do something to being able to do it), or acquiring a habit (from not doing something to doing it). For example:

せっきょくてき じぶん いけん い
① 積極的に自分から意見が言えるようになった。／(I've become) able to
express my own opinions proactively.
となり こども さいきん
② 隣 の子供は最近きちんとあいさつするようになった。／The neighbor's kid
has started greeting people properly lately.
にほんご はな
③ 日本語が話せるようになりました。／I've become able to speak Japanese.
4. 〜し、〜 <listing; coordination>
Pattern: plain sentence + し、〜
The particle "し" attaches to a clause: two or more "し"-clauses can be coordinated to list reasons;
or a single "し"-clause can cite just one reason among several.
Translate as "both...and...", "what's more...".
きょう あめ かぜ つよ やくそく
① 今日は雨だし、風も強いし、約束をキャンセルしましょうか。／today
It's raining, and the wind is strong too — let's cancel our date.
しづ ひ あ かいてき
② このアパートは静かだし、日当たりもいいし、とても快適だ。／This apartment
is quiet, and it gets good sunlight — very comfortable.
きょう こうきゅう た
③ 今日はボーナスをもらったし、高級 レストランでおいしいものを食べ
ましょう。／I got my bonus today, so let's go eat something nice at a fancy restaurant.

たい たい

たい

6. V ばいい
With a purpose or reason as premise, the conditional "V ば" followed by the adjective "いい" can be used
to give advice or state a suitable method or means — "you can...; ...will do; just..."

しごと いや
① その仕事が嫌なら、やめればいい。／If you don't like the job, just quit.
まち れきし やまだ き
② この町の歴史なら、山田さんに聞けばいいですよ。／If it's about this town's history,
you can ask Yamada.
あたま いた
③ A：頭が痛い…／My head hurts...
くすり の なお
B：この薬を飲めばいいよ。すぐ治るよ。／Take this medicine. You'll get better

7. V てみる
The te-form of a verb followed by "てみる" expresses trying something out — "try ...-ing".

いちどにほん い
① 一度日本に行ってみたいです。／I want to go to Japan at least once.
りゅうがく いちどおや そうだん
② 留学 のことはもう一度親と相談してみます。／About studying abroad, I'll try discussing it once more
with my parents.
ひと き
③ わからないことは、あの人に聞いてみればいいです。／If there's something you don't understand,
try asking that person.
8. ために＜purpose＞
Pattern: N の + ために
V る+ために
"Noun + の" or the dictionary form of a verb followed by "ために" expresses purpose. When "ために"
expresses purpose, the subjects of the two clauses must be the same, and the action in the main clause
should be a volitional verb aimed at achieving that purpose.
にほん でんとうぶんか りかい さどう なら
① 日本の伝統文化を理解するために、茶道を習いました。／
To learn about Japan's traditional culture, I studied the tea ceremony.
てんしょく めんせつ う
② 転職のために、いろいろ面接を受けた。／To change jobs, I went to various interviews.

いえ か あさ ばん はたら
③ 家を買うために、朝から晩まで働く。／To buy a house, I work from morning to night.

9. 〜ところ、〜
Pattern: V る／V ている／V た＋ところ、〜
"ところ" follows the plain verb form, "V ている", or "V た", highlighting the point in time within the
course of the action — with the nuance that the main clause's event interrupts the ongoing state of the subordinate
clause. Similar to the "V る／Vている／Vた＋ところだ" learned in Lesson 24, "V るところ" marks a point just
before the action happens, "V ているところ" a point while it is in progress, and "V たところ" a point just
after it has happened. Depending on the verb in the main clause, particles such as "に、を、へ、で"
can follow "V る／V ている／V た＋ところ".

たの はじ でんわ
① ずっと楽しみにしていたドラマが始まったところ（に）、電話がかかっ
てきた。／Just as the drama I'd been looking forward to was starting, a phone call came in.
しあい すこ お あめ ちゅうし
② その試合はもう少しで終わるところ（で）、雨で中止になった。／match; game; competition
It was just about to end when, at that very moment, it was called off due to rain.
こうじ はじ たいふう き
③ ようやく工事が始まったところ（に）、台風が来た。／Construction had finally just begun,
when the typhoon came.
10. 〜そうにもない
Pattern: V ます stem ＋そうにもない
Lesson 26 covered "〜そうだ" for appearance. The negative form of the masu-stem + "そうだ" is
"〜そうにもない"; the "に" and "も" can each be omitted, giving "〜そうにない" or "〜そうもない".

ひとり ちから かんせい
① このプロジェクトは 1人の 力 では完成できそうにない。／This project
cannot be completed by one person's strength alone.
まった べんきょう ごうかく
② 全く勉強していないので、合格しそうもない。／I haven't studied at all, so there's
no way I'll pass.

もんだい むずか ぼく
③ この問題は 難 しすぎて、僕にはわかりそうもない。／This problem is too hard;
there's no way I can understand it.
11. わ <sentence-final particle>
The sentence-final particle "わ" expresses mild emotion or surprise. The "わ" in example ① is usually
spoken with a rising intonation, mostly by older women; the "わ" in example ② can also be used by men,
but with a falling intonation.
ひと
① あら、きれいな人だわ。／Oh my, what a beautiful person.
たす
② ほんと、助かるわ。／Really, that's a big help.
